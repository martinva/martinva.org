In English. David Martín Valladolid is an economist (University of Deusto) and independent researcher in economic history and quantitative Palaeohispanic epigraphy. His work reads the Iberian lead plaques as accounting documents and compares the Iberian lexicon statistically with 107 languages. Project website: iberico.martinva.org.
